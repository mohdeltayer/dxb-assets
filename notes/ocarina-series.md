## Episode «خط الزمن»: verified facts (23 Oct 2026, 19:30Z)

- Nintendo, zelda.nintendo.com/about ("The history of Hyrule"): "At this point, time splits into two paths, one where the hero of Hyrule is victorious, and another where he falls in battle" (labels "The Hero is Defeated", "The Hero is Triumphant"); "After the events of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, the timeline splinters yet again, this time between the Child and Adult timelines" ("Child Era", "Adult Era"). Child: Majora's Mask, Twilight Princess, Four Swords Adventures. Adult ("Era Without a Hero"): The Wind Waker, Phantom Hourglass, Spirit Tracks. Defeated ("Era of Decline"): A Link to the Past ... The Legend of Zelda, Zelda II. Use Nintendo's two-step framing, not "three branches". The page does not list Breath of the Wild or Tears of the Kingdom.
- Hyrule Historia holds "the official chronology of the games" (Dark Horse); Japan release 21 Dec 2011 is secondary only.
- Iwata Asks, Majora's Mask 3D, "Make it in a Year": Iwata, "the development for Majora's Mask began when somebody requested that it be made in one year"; Aonuma, Miyamoto asked "whether we could make a game in one year if we repurpose the models"; footnote: "The story of Majora's Mask takes place a few months after the story of Ocarina of Time. Its game system and graphics were also used." Not "engine".
- Breath of the Wild: Famitsu 1 Aug 2018 (famitsu.com/news/201808/01161782.html), Aonuma: 「それはもちろん最後ですよ。でもまあ…どのラインに続く最後なのか、という話ですよね？」; Fujibayashi: 「ご想像にお任せ、じゃないですか？」.
- Stills: BotW 1920x1080 and the remake 1920x1080 and map on assets.nintendo.com (URLs in the research report of 23 Oct); zelda.nintendo.com images fail TLS verification here and are not fetched.
