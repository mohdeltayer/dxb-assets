## 2. NINTENDO: courts, takedowns, own terms
A. US default judgment, Nintendo of America v. Williams ("Archbox"). Judgment reported 25 Sep 2026.
 - US District Court, Western District of Washington, Judge Lauren King. Default judgment $4.5 million = $150,000 x 30 first-party Switch games; permanent injunction (no distributing pirated games or copy-protection circumvention software, no encouraging downloads). Defendant never answered.
 - Exact entry date NOT stated on the two pages I read (one outlet summary says 23 Sep 2026; unverified). Nintendo said nothing new; figures come from the court filings as reported.
 - OUTLET (read): https://nintendowire.com/news/2026/09/25/court-awards-4-5-million-in-damages-to-nintendo-in-case-against-reddit-moderator-archbox/ and https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/court-orders-reddit-moderator-who-widely-pirated-switch-games-to-pay-nintendo-4-5-million-in-damages/
 - Docket not read directly (CourtListener needs auth, PACER not available). Say "default judgment, per court filings reported by Nintendo Wire and VGC".
B. Netherlands, The Hague District Court, reported 11 Sep 2026. OUTLET (Nintendo Wire, read).
 - Sale of "devices designed to circumvent Nintendo's security measures" (flash-cartridge type) is unlawful. Another outlet adds EUR 10,000/day penalty capped at EUR 500,000 (not verified on a primary).
 - Nintendo statement to press, quoted by Nintendo Wire: "Nintendo's message to players is to avoid purchasing or using circumvention devices, circumvention software or unauthorized copies of video games, as this increases the risk of interfering with the functionality and experience provided by authentic Nintendo game consoles, video games and systems."
 - https://nintendowire.com/?p=221364 (published 2026-09-11)
C. US $2 million judgment, Nintendo v. Ryan Daly (Modded Hardware). Reported 8 Sep 2025 (older than 2026). OUTLET (Nintendo Wire, read): federal court in Seattle, $2 million, permanent injunction on selling or promoting modded devices; Daly had denied wrongdoing, agreed to the stipulation.

OUTLET only (godisageek, VGC): https://godisageek.com/2026/08/nintendo-401-switch-emulator-repos-dmca/ . Not read on GitHub's DMCA repository. ## 6. AI
A. Riot, "Vanguard On-Demand" post by anti-cheat lead Phillip Koskinas. PRIMARY. Date: page undated; outlets report the announcement on 24 Jun 2026 (image files dated 2026-05-26). Treat as "June 2026 (per outlets)".
 - Computer-vision cheats: "A 'pixelbot' is a computer vision cheat that injects player input for the purposes of aiming at heads or casting spells with perfect timing."
 - Warning about AI-made cheats: "As the barrier to entry on cheats is slowly lowered to anyone with a coding agent subscription, we are seeing more cheating assets than ever before (many even unique to individual users)".
 - "Today, cheaters infect about 0.7% of all PC ranked matches across both LoL or VALORANT." Also 35% of players already meet Pre-Check.
 - https://www.riotgames.com/en/news/vanguard-on-demand
B. Activision, "Ready for Launch" post, 10 Nov 2025. PRIMARY (datePublished 2025-11-10).
 - "Our aimbot detection models are trained to decide whether a player's targeting was performed by a human or an aimbot." Behavioural models look at kills/deaths/damage ratios, aiming angles, reaction times, ping.
 - "cutting median detection time to under three matches and keeping nearly 99% of Beta games cheater-free" (Activision's own claim, not independently verified).
 - https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2025/11/call-of-duty-black-ops-7-ricochet-anticheat-day-one
C. Activision Security and Enforcement Policy, PRIMARY, undated: "RICOCHET Anti-Cheat is used for data collection and machine learning to reduce cheating". https://support.activision.com/articles/call-of-duty-security-and-enforcement-policy
D. Krafton PUBG 2026 roadmap, PRIMARY (~24 Mar 2026): will "introduce AI-powered video review for more efficient pattern analysis". https://pubg.com/en/news/9856
E. Activision Season 02 post, 2 Feb 2026, PRIMARY: detections look at "input timing, consistency, and response patterns to distinguish natural human play from machine-modified input". https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/02/call-of-duty-black-ops-7-ricochet-anti-cheat-season-02

## 7. WHAT IT MEANS FOR PLAYERS (official policy)
 - Account ban: Activision policy page (PRIMARY, undated): bans "can apply across titles, including past, present, or future titles"; only permanent bans can be appealed. Black Ops 7 beta post (Sep 2025): permanent cheating bans in the beta apply across all Call of Duty titles (via search summary, not re-read).
 - Hardware ban: Activision policy (PRIMARY): "Hardware bans apply to the device and not the account"; devices with an active hardware ban "cannot be used for online gameplay". https://support.activision.com/articles/call-of-duty-security-and-enforcement-policy
 - Console/device: Nintendo (2F, 2G), Sony (3A: PlayStation Device can be restricted), Microsoft (3B: profile or device, licences forfeited).
 - Steam: account restriction or termination without notice (4A).
 - PC security requirement as consequence: Activision Season 04 post, 4 Jun 2026 (PRIMARY, datePublished 2026-06-04): players who fail Microsoft Azure Attestation (TPM 2.0 + Secure Boot) are limited to Nuketown 24/7 in Black Ops 7 and Battle Royale Casual in Warzone. https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/06/call-of-duty-black-ops-7-warzone-ricochet-anti-cheat-season-04
 - Same post: "Nearly two-thirds of players temporarily banned for using these [input] devices don't return to them" (Activision's claim). Usable as a figure with the device names left out.
