The X originality rule matters for us: Original Content Rewards counts
"commentary and analysis on news or cultural moments" but excludes
content that "aggregates other creators' work without adding meaningful
perspective" and content reuploaded from other platforms. DXB-KNIGHT's
voiced posts and analysis cards fit; Digital Lounge's straight news may
not, and a Reel reposted from Instagram could count as reuploaded. The
supported-country list for the new programme could not be read (X's help

## Rules before any paid or promotional post

- UAE Advertiser Permit (UAE Media Council), in force since 1 Feb 2026:
  needed for any promotional content published from the UAE, paid or
  not, whatever the follower count. AED 370 per cycle; fines up to
  AED 500,000. Get it before the first sponsored post or affiliate link.
- Saudi Arabia: Mawthooq is for residents; a non-resident works with
  Saudi brands through a Mawthooq-registered agency.
- Label every paid post (#إعلان on Digital Lounge, "Ad" on DXB-KNIGHT),
  and keep sponsors off news cards unless the card says so. Needs an
  agreed chip before it is used (the chip lists refuse anything else).
- Editorial line: no paid news, no paid verdicts. A sponsor buys a
  labelled slot, never a story.

## The plan

Platform payouts are small or out of reach for months, so revenue comes
in this order: brand and sponsor deals first (they need reach, not
programme thresholds), YouTube second (the only full programme open in
the region), X rewards on DXB-KNIGHT third, TikTok and Instagram payouts
when they open here.

### Phase 0, now to mid October: set up, no revenue

1. Check each in-app location above and screenshot the eligibility
   screens into `analytics/` so the plan works from real numbers.
2. Confirm DXB-KNIGHT's Premium and pull its 90-day verified impressions
   from Creator Studio.
3. Apply for the UAE Advertiser Permit (AED 370).
4. Keep logging Reel and Short numbers in `analytics/digital-lounge.md`;
   they become the media kit.

### Phase 1, October to December: reach the thresholds that are reachable

- YouTube, long-form route. 3M Shorts views in 90 days is out of reach
  for now; watch hours are not. Add one Arabic long video a week (a
  6 to 10 minute weekly news roundup built from the week's Digital
  Lounge stories and trailers). 3,000 hours in 12 months is about
  1,200 views a week at 3 minutes watched each. Shorts keep feeding
  subscribers (500 for Tier 1, 1,000 for Tier 2).
- DXB-KNIGHT on X: the growth points already agreed (hero posts,
  analysis cards while the topic is live, his takes) are exactly what
  Original Content Rewards pays for. Target 500 verified followers and
  500,000 verified impressions in 90 days.
- Digital Lounge Instagram and TikTok: follows, not views, are the gap
  (0 from 1,014 viewers on the Minecraft Reel). The ideas on file in
  `analytics/digital-lounge.md` (bio, pinned Reels, shorter Reels, end
  frame) are the levers when he wants to use them.

### Phase 2, from about 5,000 views per Reel on average: first sponsors

- Media kit (one page, Arabic and English): accounts, 30-day views,
  average views per Reel and Short, audience countries, best posts.
- What to sell, all labelled:
  - "Presented by" on the weekly YouTube roundup and the monthly
    release calendar (the lookout).
  - A sponsored Reel for a game launch (publisher or regional
    distributor), clearly marked.
  - Event coverage packages (esports and regional events).
- Who to approach: regional publisher and platform teams (PlayStation
  Middle East, Xbox Arabia), regional distributors and retailers
  (gaming stores and electronics chains), esports organisers and
  federations, gaming peripheral brands. Saudi brands through a
  Mawthooq-registered agency.
- Affiliate: Amazon.ae Associates for hardware and games mentioned in
  posts, links in bio and YouTube descriptions only (Instagram caption
  links do not click). Needs the permit first. Keep it off news posts.

### Phase 3, 2027: platform payouts

- YouTube Tier 1 then Tier 2; memberships once Tier 1 opens.
- X rewards on DXB-KNIGHT once eligible.
- TikTok Creator Rewards and Instagram programmes if they open in the
  UAE; check the in-app locations each quarter.
