# Scout proposals for after the trial (report due 5 Oct 2026)

Drafted 2 Oct 2026 from the newsroom review Mohammad approved
(`newsroom/README.md`, item 7). Nothing here goes to the scout until he
has read the trial report and agreed. The ground rules of
`scout-optimize-prompt.md` still apply: additive only, no new cost, no
logins or bot-check workarounds, and an addition never costs a digest.

## 1. Urgent flag in the subject

When a sheet carries a major unscheduled item (a surprise reveal or
shadow-drop, a delay or cancellation of a major game, a platform holder's
price or policy change, a studio closure), the subject starts "🔴 URGENT:"
plus the item in a few words. His mail app then shows it on the lock
screen. No more than one or two a day. Every other sheet keeps its
current subject.

## 2. A role on every source

Each line in a sheet already names its source. Add the source's role, so
a sweep can see the chain at a glance:

- official: the company's own channel
- original reporting: an outlet or journalist breaking it themselves
- technical: Digital Foundry and similar analysis
- outlet report: an outlet reporting with no primary found (already
  labelled in the trial)
- rumour: leakers and unnamed sources
- radar: an outlet rewriting someone else

When several outlets carry the same item, name the earliest one and add
"(also: …)" instead of filing it once per outlet.

## 3. Failing sources

When a source in the map fails three sweeps in a row (blocked, empty,
moved), say so once under Skips, with the URL and the error, instead of
skipping it silently. One line, until it recovers.

## 4. Show days

When a confirmed show is under way (Nintendo Direct, State of Play, Xbox
showcase, The Game Awards, a major publisher showcase), send one extra
sheet within about an hour of the end, listing every announcement with
its official page. This is within the current schedule if the next
hourly sheet falls in that hour; otherwise it is one added run, which
needs his OK under rule 3.

## For the trial report

Questions to answer from the report before any of this is sent:

- Did the new sources (Arabic outlets, Japanese feeds, Gulf official
  sources) file anything that made a post?
- How many refiles still slipped through?
- Did any run go long or miss a digest?
