# Reel audit: strongest and weakest openings, 11 to 24 Oct 2026

Digital Lounge Instagram Reels, published 11 Oct to 24 Oct 2026. Data pulled 24 Oct about 10:15Z (14:15 Dubai).

## Coverage

- Source: Publer post insights for the Digital Lounge Instagram account. Publer reads the account's own insights, so it lists Reels from 11 Oct onwards, including those posted through Postiz before 22 Oct: 286 entries for the period. (The pages repeat some posts when views tie, so 286 is an upper bound.)
- Ranking set: Reels at least 24 hours old at the pull, so everything published after 14:15 Dubai on 23 Oct is left out. The Marathon correction notice (14 Oct, 130 views) is left out too, because it is not a story. Ranked by video views.
- Matched to `templates/assets/format-ledger.json` and `newsroom/stories/` by publish time and caption. The first frames were taken from the published mp4s in git history (`cards/2026-10/`). Strips exist for all 20.
- Metrics Publer provides: views, reach, likes, comments, shares, saves, engagement rate, reach rate. **Metrics Publer does not provide:** skip rate, average watch time, 3-second retention, total watch time, replays, follows from the post, follower and non-follower split. Those have to come from Instagram's own insights.
- Skin: every Reel in the period uses the house look (no game-skin kit has gone live). Before 20 Oct the ledger leaves skin blank.
- Sound: taken from story notes where they record it. Where they do not (marked "meas."), it is inferred from the audio: a speech transcript and the high-frequency content, checked against Reels whose sound is known.
- Genre: from the story file. Where the story file has none (marked *), I assigned the nearest house genre.

## Shape of the data

The views split into two groups. About 80 Reels reached roughly 850 to 4,200 views. Most of the rest sit on a plateau of about 120 to 300 views, with reach of 110 to 135, which looks like delivery to the existing audience only. The median is about 200 views. Roughly the bottom 40 Reels all fall between 119 and 141 views, so ranks 4 to 10 of the bottom ten are separated from the next 30 Reels by only a handful of views. Three Reels fell below even the plateau, and all three went out on 21 Oct: AION 2, Battlefield 1 and Civilization VI. The Battlefield 1 post was v2, reposted after v1 was deleted for damaged footage.

## Reading

- **Weak openings:** none of the bottom ten opens on a face or a character large in frame. Four open on our own card or graphic (AION 2, Steam, Alinea, Apex), and four more on a static or distant picture: Pikachu as a still card, a product shot, a wide pixel scene, an empty desert. Card-led templates (numbers, explainer) account for 4 of the bottom ten and none of the top ten.
- **Strong openings:** seven of the top ten open on a recognisable face or character filling the 9:16 frame (Maomao, the Edgerunners eye, the assassin, Monokuma, Mario, the Eternia hero, the Samson hero). One more opens on a single clear object, the ship. Both quote Reels in the top ten opened on footage, not on a speaker card.
- **Sound does not separate the groups.** Our bed carries two top-ten Reels (jailbreak, Liquid Swords) and three bottom-ten ones. Voiced trailer audio appears on both sides too.
- **Strong topic, weak opening:** Pokémon TCG Pocket (129) and ONE PIECE (126) have large fanbases, but they opened on a still card and a wide pixel scene. Civilization VI names Saladin in the headline but shows a rocket. Both "on this day" Reels with a Gulf angle (Battlefield 1, Civilization VI) are in the bottom three.
- **Weak topic that still did well:** the Danganronpa PC specs story (2,382) and the Liquid Swords bankruptcy (2,053) are dry stories, but both opened on a character. Warframe (2,483) opened on a planet with no character; its headline carried a "one time only" deadline, and it drew the second-most shares in the period.
- **Timing:** four of the bottom ten went out between 01:30 and 03:46 Dubai, against one of the top ten (04:31). Five of the bottom ten went out on 21 Oct, including the three Reels below the plateau. The top ten still has two from that day (Apothecary, Crimson Desert), so the date alone does not explain them.
- **Top topics:** the top ten leans on anime and anime-style games (Apothecary, Edgerunners, Danganronpa, Tales of Eternia).
