# Sources that count (release 2026-11-10 r4, coverage over caution)

Mohammad, 9 Nov 2026 evening, after Production 2's scout dropped A Little to the Left as "no source found" while the
publisher's press release and Nintendo Everything both carried it: "I want to fix the strict rules in production 2
because it's limiting the coverage... It could've checked Nintendo Everything to verify."

The same hosts are in `newsroom/scout_file.py` (`PRIMARY_HOSTS`, `CREDIBLE_HOSTS`), which refuses a no_source
rejection while one of them is in hand.

## Primary (chip رسمي or جديد; a fact on screen comes from here)

- The publisher's, studio's or platform holder's own site, newsroom, blog or store page (Nintendo eShop, PlayStation
  Store, Microsoft Store, Steam store and Steam news, Epic).
- Their official YouTube channels and official X, Instagram or Discord posts. An official video is a primary for what
  it shows and says: a date read from a trailer or a showcase is a fact, with its time zone.
- Press wires carrying the company's own release: GamesPress, PR Newswire, Business Wire, GlobeNewswire, Cision.
- Investor relations pages, filings and earnings materials; rating boards (ESRB, PEGI, Korea, Taiwan, Australia).

## Credible outlets (a source: chip تقرير at worst, naming the outlet, «لم تؤكد [الشركة] الخبر بعد»)

Nintendo Everything, Nintendo Life, Gematsu, VGC, IGN, GameSpot, Eurogamer, PC Gamer, PCGamesN, Polygon, The Verge,
Kotaku, Insider Gaming, Bloomberg, Reuters, Nikkei, Axios, Famitsu, 4Gamer, Automaton, Gamekult, Push Square, Pure Xbox,
Windows Central, GamesIndustry.biz, The Game Business, Rock Paper Shotgun, Digital Foundry, Siliconera, Destructoid,
GamesRadar, DualShockers, Game Rant, Pocket Tactics, Dexerto, VideoGamer, TweakTown.

One credible outlet is enough. When the outlet quotes the primary (a press release, a video, a post), read and link
the primary; when it cannot be opened, publish on the outlet's report and say so.

## Arabic outlets and relays

Never a lead on their own (17 Oct): trace the story to its first report. Traced to a primary or a credible outlet, it
is that source's story. Untraced after the search below, it is no_source.

## The search before any no_source

1. A web search for the story itself: the game's name and the claim ("A Little to the Left" Switch 2 Edition).
2. The outlet the lead came from (the digest's link), read in full.
3. The press wires and the publisher's own channels (site, YouTube, X).

A newsroom front page that does not show the story, or answers 403, 404 or 503, proves nothing. The scout proposes
no_source with what it searched; the editor runs its own search and confirms (`"confirmed_by": "editor"`).

## Not reasons

Single outlet; no primary read; no written primary; the date only in a video; an Arabic relay that was traced; waiting
for a statement; age under 48 hours when Arabic outlets have not carried it; no trailer (stills or the showcase
layout); variety (it decides the order, not whether a story runs). Excluded sources (8 Nov) stay excluded.
