# Editorial memory (adv-20261102-story-brief, part 3; Mohammad, 2 Nov 2026)

One entry per hold, correction or praise from Mohammad, newest first. Each entry: date (Dubai), the Reel, its story type,
what was wrong or right, and the rule in one line. A story brief pulls every entry of the same story type before the
angle is written. Add the entry the same day; when the rule belongs in `newsroom/reel-check.md`, add it there too.

Story types: INTERVIEW, DELAY, OUT NOW, NUMBERS, TRAILER OR FIRST LOOK, PEOPLE, EVENT, REPORT, LIST, SERIES (add as needed).

## 2026-11-02 · Ocarina of Time developers interview (Ask the Developer Vol. 24) · INTERVIEW · held
Wrong:
- interview speech ran under the Arabic text, so the text read as a translation of it;
- the story was a list of facts, not a hook;
- the text drifted on screen;
- the Triforce was half-cropped;
- the Reel said an interview exists but left out what was said in it.
Rule: an interview Reel carries the two or three tidbits that are news, as short attributed quotes, never only "an interview was published" and never the whole interview.
Sources: adv-20261102-reel-checklist, adv-20261102-story-brief; story file 2026-11-02-zelda-ocarina-developers-interview-ask-the-developer-24.

## 2026-11-02 · Suda51 on Grasshopper's next game (Famitsu interview) · INTERVIEW · accepted
Right: a single interview with new plans as the news (a dark story game he will write, a Silver Case revival "possible", No More Heroes' 20th anniversary in 2027 with Marvelous), attributed to Famitsu; the old split from NetEase kept as background only.
Rule: lead an interview with what is new in it, attributed, and keep known background to one line.
Sources: prod-20261102-review-0630; story file 2026-11-02-suda51-travis-touchdown-will-return-famitsu.

## 2026-10-30 · One Piece x Fortnite Treasure Hunters · TRAILER OR FIRST LOOK · held

Rule: footage fills the frame, or goes full frame with designed bands; never a blurred fill, and never a headline over a face (now in CLAUDE.md "Full frame").

## 2026-10-28 · Indie World (showcase Reels) · EVENT · held

Rule: a showcase Reel shows the announced games moving; the logo is a badge, not the picture.
Sources: adv-20261102-borrow-openmontage (calibration set); story file 2026-10-27-nintendo-indie-world-oct-28.
