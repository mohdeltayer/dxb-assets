# Editorial memory (adv-20261102-story-brief, part 3; Mohammad, 2 Nov 2026)

One entry per hold, correction or praise from Mohammad, newest first. Each entry: date (Dubai), the Reel, its story type,
what was wrong or right, and the rule in one line. A story brief pulls every entry of the same story type before the
angle is written. Add the entry the same day; when the rule belongs in `newsroom/reel-check.md`, add it there too.

Story types: INTERVIEW, DELAY, OUT NOW, NUMBERS, TRAILER OR FIRST LOOK, PEOPLE, EVENT, REPORT, LIST, SERIES (add as needed).

## 2026-11-04 · NIKKE PC launcher notice (Reel of 11:31 Dubai) · SECURITY NOTICE / CARDS ONLY · deleted by Mohammad
Wrong: a Reel of text cards on our bed with no picture of the game ("ماذا حدث" and "تعديل محتمل" panels on a flat ground); 58 views on Instagram, then deleted.
Rule: every Reel shows the game: official footage first, or official key art and screenshots; a notice, a figure or a warning is a line or strip over the game, never the whole picture. Cards only is not a Reel.
Sources: Mohammad, 4 Nov 2026, Instagram grid screenshot; commitments row nikke-pc-launcher-tampering-notice-nov-4.

## 2026-11-04 · Unreal Engine 6 without Blueprints (Reel of 12:02 Dubai) · ENGINE / DEVELOPER-TOOL NEWS · deleted by Mohammad
Wrong: engine news for developers as a text-card Reel with no game footage; 6 views on Instagram, then deleted. It was also a single-outlet report (4Gamer) taken as a gap fill.
Rule: engine and developer-tool news does not fill a gap; it goes to the review shortlist for Mohammad, and runs only if it matters to players and shows a game. Pick stories that matter to players.
