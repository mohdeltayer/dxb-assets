# Events calendar

Confirmed dates only, each with where it was confirmed and when that was
read. Times go in Dubai time with the source's own zone beside them;
"time not announced" until it is. Undated shows stay at the bottom and are
watched for an announcement (they are usually announced a day or two
ahead). Release dates live in the monthly lookout (`templates/ahead.py`),
not here.

On a show day: capture each announcement as it lands (game, type,
platforms, date or window, trailer, official page), then check platforms,
dates, prices and exclusivity against the official page or press release
before posting. Reminders before a show only when Mohammad asks.
