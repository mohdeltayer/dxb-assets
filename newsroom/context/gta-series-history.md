## Units (Take-Two's figures are sell-in: units shipped to retail and sold digitally, as Take-Two reports them)
- GTA V: "has sold-in over 225 million units worldwide" (Take-Two Form 10-K for the year to 31 Mar 2026, filed 2026-05-22,
  sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/946581/000162828026037434/ttwo-20260331.htm). Earlier: "has sold over 190 million units to
  date" (Rockstar/Take-Two release announcing GTA VI, 4 Dec 2023). The fiscal Q1 2027 release (7 Aug 2026) gives no unit figure.
- Grand Theft Auto series: "has sold-in over 465 million units worldwide" (same 10-K); "over 470 million units sold-in
  worldwide" (Take-Two, pre-order release, 24 Jun 2026; the newest figure).
- Red Dead Redemption 2: "has sold-in more than 80 million units worldwide" (same 10-K).
- GTA IV first week: "approximately six million units purchased by consumers globally in the first week" (FY2008 10-K).
- Not found in a primary: GTA V's "$1 billion in three days" (2013); do not print it.

## GTA VI windows and delays (Take-Two releases)
- 4 Dec 2023: announced, "coming to PlayStation 5 ... and Xbox Series X|S ... in 2025".
- 6 Nov 2024 (fiscal Q2 2025 results): "Fall of calendar 2025".
- 2 May 2025: "which was expected to launch in Fall of 2025, is now planned for release on May 26, 2026".
- 6 Nov 2025 (fiscal Q2 2026 results): "Grand Theft Auto VI now launching November 19, 2026".
- Repeated since: 3 Feb 2026, 21 and 22 May 2026, 24 Jun 2026 (pre-orders), 7 Aug 2026.
- Rockstar Newswire's own delay posts were not readable on 8 Nov; if an episode quotes Rockstar, read the post first.
