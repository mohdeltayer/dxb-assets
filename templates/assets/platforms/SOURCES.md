# Platform logos: sources

Each `<slug>.png` is the mark in single-colour white (#FFFFFF) on a transparent
background, trimmed to its bounds, 160 px high. The alpha channel carries the
shape and its anti-aliased edges; the RGB is white everywhere. `<slug>.svg` is
kept where the holder serves the mark as SVG (made white where it was not, as
noted). SVG marks were rendered at 2400 px high with cairosvg, then trimmed and
downscaled (Lanczos) to 160 px. Nothing was redrawn and no shape was changed.

All fetched 24 Oct 2026. Every file is a trademark of its holder; each holder's
own usage rules apply (see notes).

## Not sourced

- **Xbox One (xbox-one).** xbox.com no longer shows an Xbox One logo (its Xbox One pages carry only the Xbox and Game Pass marks), news.xbox.com and Microsoft's partner brand toolkit are not reachable, and the Commons files (File:Xbox one logo.svg, File:Xbox One logo wordmark.svg) cite an imgur image and a third-party manual PDF, not an official page, and do not state they are the official file. The current Xbox logo (sphere + XBOX, https://uhf.microsoft.com/images/xbox/RW8TP2.png, 410x124 white) is official but is the Xbox brand mark, not Xbox One, so it was not substituted.
- **Mac (mac).** Apple publishes no Mac platform mark. The Apple logo is the only candidate, and Apple's App Store Marketing Guidelines (https://developer.apple.com/app-store/marketing/guidelines/) say "Don't use the standalone Apple logo" and "Never use the Apple logo in place of the word Apple". Suggest writing "Mac" as text.
- **App Store (app-store).** Apple only supplies the App Store badge ("Download on the App Store", black or white). Its guidelines state the badge's border "is part of the badge artwork and should not be modified", so a single-colour white version cannot be made without altering it. tools.applemarketingtools.com was also unreachable (proxy 502) on 24 Oct. If a badge is wanted, use Apple's unaltered black badge from that page.
- **Meta Quest (meta-quest).** meta.com, about.meta.com/brand/resources and developers.meta.com (brand guidelines) all return HTTP 400 to automated requests, and the newsroom media gallery (about.fb.com/media-gallery) needs a Facebook login. Commons' File:Meta Quest logo.svg gives only "http://oculus.com" as source and does not state it is the official file, so it does not meet the bar. Meta's developer brand page also says the logo must not be "modified, or altered in any way".
