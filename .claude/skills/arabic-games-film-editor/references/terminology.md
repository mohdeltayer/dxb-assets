# Context-sensitive terminology

These are editorial starting points, not verified publisher terminology or a user-approved glossary. Explicit user preferences and supplied official localization take priority.

| Source term | Suggested treatment | Important distinction |
|---|---|---|
| First impressions | انطباعات أولية | Do not present an early impression as a final review. |
| Gameplay | أسلوب اللعب | Do not narrow it to combat unless the source refers to combat. |
| Combat | القتال or نظام القتال, as appropriate | Not interchangeable with all gameplay. |
| Visuals | الرسوم or المظهر البصري, depending on context | Do not add “art direction” unless intended. |
| Pacing | الإيقاع, with an explicit referent when needed | This is not necessarily frame rate. |
| Frame rate | معدل الإطارات | Preserve numbers and units. |
| Locked 30 fps | معدل ثابت يبلغ 30 إطارًا في الثانية | Do not infer stability from a mere 30 fps target. |
| Handheld mode | الوضع المحمول | Distinguish from docked/TV mode. |
| Docked mode | وضع الإرساء or وضع التلفاز, according to context | Follow the chosen house term consistently. |
| Internal resolution | دقة التصيير الداخلية or دقة العرض الداخلية, according to context | Distinguish the internal resolution from the final output. |
| Upscaled from X to Y | مُرقّاة من X إلى Y, or a clear equivalent | Preserve which number is the source and which is the output. |
| Dungeon | Resolve from the game and its accepted terminology | Do not automatically replace it with a prison, an underground passage, ruins or a general exploration zone. |
| Boss | Resolve from context and the house glossary | Distinguish a game enemy encounter from a workplace manager. |
| One playable character | شخصية واحدة قابلة للعب, when that is explicitly meant | One character, one campaign, one class and one route are not interchangeable. |

## Handling unresolved terms

Check source context and any supplied glossary first. Do not invent an official Arabic name. When the distinction materially affects the translation, ask one targeted question or retain the source term with a short note outside the translation. Do not make up a location's architecture to find a convenient Arabic equivalent.

## Building a house glossary

For each term, record: English term; approved Arabic equivalent; game or context; excluded meanings; approval date. A term may need different entries for different contexts. Treat suggestions as unapproved until the user accepts them.
