# Collocations (المتلازمات اللفظية): calque → natural

The core rule: **do not build an Arabic collocation by calquing English.** English "make / do /
take / give / pay / play" must not become عمل / أخذ / أعطى. Each concept has an established
Arabic verb or adjective — use it. Choosing the wrong collocate is one of the loudest
translationese signals even when every word is individually correct.

1. Verb + noun

## 3. Noun + noun and fixed phrases

- replaces → يحلّ محلّ (not يأخذ مكان)
- a glimmer of hope → بصيص أمل
- outbreak of war → نشوب / اندلاع الحرب
- outbreak of disease → تفشّي المرض
- peak of the crisis → ذروة الأزمة
- with great difficulty → بشقّ الأنفس
- works as a translator → يعمل مترجمًا (a ḥāl), **not** يعمل كمترجم

## 4. Idioms (both directions) — translate by sense, never by image

English → Arabic:
- it's raining cats and dogs → تمطر بغزارة (not إنها تمطر قططًا وكلابًا)
- piece of cake → أمر سهل / أمر هيّن
- break the ice → يكسر الجمود / يكسر حاجز الصمت
- once in a blue moon → في النادر / بين الحين والآخر
- cost an arm and a leg → كلّف ثمنًا باهظًا
- kick the bucket → فارق الحياة

Arabic → English (render the meaning, not the literal words):
- على عيني وراسي → "gladly / with pleasure"
- الطيور على أشكالها تقع → "birds of a feather flock together"
- على قدر لحافك مدّ رجليك → "live within your means"

When no equivalent idiom exists in the target language, **paraphrase the sense** plainly.

## 5. Verb–preposition government (the right حرف جر)

Wrong-preposition substitution is one of the largest MT error classes. Watch these (formal MSA):

- رغب **في** = "to desire"  vs  رغب **عن** = "to be averse to" (opposite meanings!)
- بحث **عن** = "to search for"  vs  بحث **في** = "to examine / look into"
- أثّر **في** (formal) = "to affect"  (على is common in journalism but في is the classical norm)
- اعتمد **على** = "to rely on"
- حصل **على** = "to obtain" (✗ حصل جائزة)
- متزوج **من** (✗ متزوج لـ)
- قادر **على**
- يفتقر **إلى**
- "wait for" = انتظر (transitive, no preposition; ✗ انتظر لـ)

**Rule:** two verbs that govern different prepositions cannot share one object —
✗ موافقته على وتأييده للقرار → ✓ موافقته على القرار وتأييده له.
