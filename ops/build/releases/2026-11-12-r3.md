# Release 2026-11-12 r3: sound by code

Card adv-20261112-sound-by-code, approved by Mohammad 12 Nov 2026 ("the same bed on most news, trailer sound only
sometimes"). Evidence on the card: of 17 footage news Reels on 10 and 11 Nov, 7 had no trailer track; five in a row on
11 Nov; templates/assets/bed-ledger.json had no entry since 9 Nov, because server renders never committed it and jobs
named a fixed `sound`. Live on merge.

## Changed paths

- templates/buildgate.py: `sound_problems` (in problems()), `footage_job`, VIDEO, BED_TEMPLATES (numbers, stills,
  retro, storybeat), BED_KINDS (sales, results). A job with a video shot and no `track` (missing, null or "bed") is
  refused unless "bed_reason" (20 characters or more) names the trailers checked, or the template, kind, tribute or
  "legal": true takes our bed.
- templates/render_job.py: `sound_for` picks the sound on the server: with a track, the trailer (label "trailer" or
  "trailer-voiced"); without one, the chip's flavour (ig_ar.SOUNDS), the tribute's neon_c, a named occasion or retro
  flavour, else the everyday bed rested longest in the ledger whatever the job says. Prints "render_job: sound: ..." and
  writes the ledger entry to $RUNNER_TEMP/bed.json.
- templates/sonic.py: `ledger_add` and `sonic.py ledger-add <bed.json>`.
- templates/qc.py: `sound_of` (qc.json "sound": trailer, trailer-voiced, bed:<flavour> with the bed_reason) and
  `qc.py sound-entry` (sound and footage on the render-times entry).
- .github/workflows/render-live.yml and render.yml: the bed ledger entry is committed with the render; render-live also
  writes sound and footage onto the render-times entry.
- ops/backoffice/keeper/workorder.py: `bed_alarms`: three footage Reels in a row on a bed in 24 hours is a work-order
  alarm.
- templates/t0make.py: a trailer with no audio stream gets track "bed" and its bed_reason written for it (the track was
  already set from the first shot's source second).
- templates/test_soundcode.py (9 tests); CLAUDE.md, STYLE.md, ops/production-setup.md line 13.

## Tests

All suites green. The commit-step commands run on fixtures: `qc.py sound-entry` wrote "bed:neon_d" and footage true onto
the last render-times entry; `sonic.py ledger-add` appended the entry. Two new everyday-bed Reels in a row take
different beds (test_soundcode). Parity: a job with a trailer track renders exactly as before (sound_for returns the
job's own `sound`); only jobs on our bed change, from the fixed bed they named to the rotated one.
