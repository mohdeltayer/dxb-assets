## Changed paths

- templates/render_job.py: `card_line_problems` in the text check (rule `card_line`: over a -gfx- card a story line is
  one visual line and clear of the card's text box, 8 px margin; shots under a HyperFrames insert are left out);
  `card_art_check` in check() (still-ground art 720 px or more on its short side; a logofact logo is a cut-out with at
  least 5% transparency); `card_stages` (the card's stages for numgfx).
- templates/cardgfx.py: `animated`, `stages`, `_counted` (count-up over 0.9 s, ease out, in the figure's own format;
  dates, years, times, scores and ratios never count), ranking rows not yet revealed are not drawn (no faint slots),
  a countdown split over two cards carries on from the first; `fit=` keeps the figure's size during the count-up.
- templates/numgfx.py: `slide()` takes a staged graphic [(png, from second)]; `shots()` passes it through.
- templates/qc.py: `findings_from_notes` (lifeless over, close, length under 12 s, collision), verdict fail on any
  finding, `look_frames` for numbers, list, explainer, quote and edition (three 360 px frames), `QC_JSON:` on fail.
- templates/presubmit.py: `qc_of`; a director PASS is refused on a qc fail, and needs `--looked "<one line>"` when the
  qc lists look frames.
- .github/workflows/render-live.yml: look frames committed with the Reel; on a fail verdict the Reel, strip, sheet and
  qc.json are committed as evidence, the run fails with the findings in its summary, and the Publer upload does not run.
- templates/test_qcfail.py (13 tests); CLAUDE.md and STYLE.md section.
