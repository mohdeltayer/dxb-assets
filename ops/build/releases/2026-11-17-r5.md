## What runs
- The editor writes one slip per story in dxb-queue `desk/slips/<YYYY-MM-DD-slug>.json`: id, label on the ladder, sources
  (the primary for official labels; an angle for تقرير and تسريب), first report, credit, footage handle (steam:<appid>, a
  Nintendo US product page, an IGN video page or a playstation.com game page), headline, spoken paragraph, Instagram, X
  and Threads text, tier, expiry. Format and refusals in `templates/slip.py`.
- `.github/workflows/slip.yml` (workflow_dispatch: slip, lane live|test) reads it, resolves the official footage (the
  footage resolver, three candidates; the first clean one leads, the others ride along as spare footage), writes the
  story file and the job, runs the mechanical text fixes and the text check, searches the ledger, commits story, job and
  result (`newsroom/slips/<id>.json`), and starts the render: render-live.yml for live (jobs/live, the server, Publer media
  upload as today), render.yml for test (jobs/test, cards/test, nothing uploaded). A slip that cannot be built is
  committed as failed with its reasons and nothing renders.
- The job: trailer template, a 3.8 s hook, every beat of the spoken paragraph across as many server-picked shots as it
  needs (each 2.2 to 2.8 s; trailers' clean stretches run about 2.6 to 3.3 s), one credit over every shot, the trailer's
  sound from the first picked shot (`track_from_first_shot`), and `"split_fallback": "bed"`: when the audio split refuses
  the trailer mix, `render_job.bed_under` lays the rotated everyday bed under the body at the old level, keeps the end
  card, and qc's sound reads `bed:<flavour> (trailer sound refused: <reason>)`. An automatic line never holds a story on
  a sound refusal.
- Mechanical checks in the slip: label ladder, primary source, angle, expiry, tier, no em dash, Western digits, Arabic
  first word on every paragraph, no hashtags on X and Threads, the Instagram hashtag line, Threads length, no + or % sign
  in headline or spoken text, people stories refused (they need a photo window). Company and studio names in brackets are
  checked against names-ar and the glossary; an unknown one is flagged on the result, never guessed.

What the first runs found, fixed in this release before the four landed: one shot per beat ran shorter than its line
(reading pace) or longer than 4 s (pace); a single trailer ran out of clean stretches; two trailer mixes were refused by
the split (leftover 18.3% and 7.2%) and the sound guard stopped the job; one visual line ran out of the safe zone (the
wrap would not break near a number). Text an editor still has to catch: «اللعبة الجديدة+» (now refused by the slip) and
a bare «تصل» on a DLC headline (the release-verb rule; for the review step in Phase 3).

## Open, in order (before the slip lane carries live news)
1. Sound: three of four fell back to our bed, which the 23 Oct rule says must be rare on footage. The automatic shots come
   from moments all over the trailer, so the trailer mix only fits after the split, and the split refuses effects-led
   mixes. Next: autoshots in step with the track (shots taken from the track's own seconds, unclean seconds skipped), so
   the trailer's own mix plays with no split; the bed only when that fails too.
2. Bed rotation: parallel jobs read the same ledger and all three took neon; the pick moves to the server's committed
   ledger with the job name as tie-break.
3. Length: Black Mesa ran 30.3 s (seven beats); a slip over five beats is flagged to the editor.
