# Release 2026-11-12 r4: scout verdict lines on the work order

Card adv-20261111-scout-verdicts-on-work-order, approved by Mohammad. Since the 23:48 Dubai digest of 10 Nov the Grok
scout adds VERDICT, LABEL, FACTS, FOOTAGE and NOT CONFIRMED to every NEW item that is not minor. Live on merge; the
server part starts reading once the key file below is installed (until then the work order says so).

## Changed paths

- ops/backoffice/scout/verdicts.py (new): reads the digest inbox read only (lists the last 3 hours of "DXB digest"
  messages and fetches their text; never marks, moves or sends), parses every item, keeps those with verdict lines,
  opens each Primary (else the outlet report) and checks every FACT's numbers and most of its names on the page:
  source_confirmed, source_unreadable (paywall, X, YouTube, 403, under 400 characters) or mismatch (facts listed).
  Label: never stronger than the scout's; رسمي stays only when source_confirmed on a company page, else تقرير; a scout
  CONFIRMED on a page the server could not open is تقرير; DENIED leads are listed as denied, never built. Bare items pass
  through as today. Writes leads and the day's measure (leads, source_confirmed).
- ops/backoffice/keeper/server_tick.sh: at the dispatch minutes, runs verdicts.py when /etc/dxb-scout/agentmail.key is
  readable and sends up to 8 leads in the keeper-server dispatch (`scout`); without the key it sends
  {"unavailable": "key not installed"}.
- ops/backoffice/keeper/workorder.py: `scout_verdicts`; section 4 of the work order lists each lead (label to use, what
  the check found, link, facts, footage, not confirmed), the denied ones, and the measure.
- ops/backoffice/scout/test_verdicts.py (6 tests); CLAUDE.md, STYLE.md, ops/production-setup.md line 14.

## Tests

All suites green. On a fixture digest in the scout's format: a PlayStation Blog item confirmed on the page keeps رسمي; a
Bloomberg item the scout marked CONFIRMED but the server cannot open becomes تقرير; a DENIED item is listed as denied; a
bare minor item is not listed; a page whose date differs from the FACT is a mismatch and drops to تقرير. The work order
renders the leads and "1 of 3 leads source_confirmed". Today's digests (14:27, 15:23, 16:27 Dubai) carry only minor
items, which have no verdict lines, so a live list will show leads from the next NEW item on.
