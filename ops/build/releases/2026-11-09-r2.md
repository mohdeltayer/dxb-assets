## Merged (live on merge, no approval gate)
- Showcase layout in reel.py (CLAUDE.md "Full frame", point 5; Mohammad 8 Nov). A job sets `"layout": "showcase"` and
  `"showcase_reason"` (five words or more naming the case: the trailer itself is the news, or the source fails the
  quality gate enlarged with no better copy; a bare true or a vague reason stops). The footage plays uncropped in a
  16:9 box as wide as fits between the bands (the frame width with a one-line headline), on the flat ground, no blur;
  the chip and headline stay in the top band for the whole Reel; the lines (56 px), the strip as a key facts row
  (40 px, down to 32) and the credits (or source and date) are text in the bottom band. The lines run back to back
  from frame 0 to the end card, so no band is ever empty. Nothing is enlarged, so the rows, zoom and block checks of
  the quality gate do not apply (bitrate flags still print). Cards, person windows, products, the storybook page, the
  design pass and the tribute variant stop the render. The text check draws the showcase lines at their real size.
- No empty band (fillcheck). A band-layout picture track whose band is empty after the headline fails whatever
  lowres_ok says (keep the headline with persist.head, or use the showcase layout). render_job reads the finished Reel
  of every band job: a run of empty rows over 160 px inside the safe zone, at the first frame or the middle of any
  shot, fails the render before anything is committed.
- Numbers on the game's art by default (adv-20261108-numbers-cards-grid items 2 and 3). `-gfx-` cards take
  `ground_footage` or the new `ground_still` (key art, numgfx 'still' ground); the plain rotating ground needs
  `"ground": "rotate"` with `"ground_reason"`. The cover may not be a card on the plain ground or a logo plate alone:
  buildgate refuses it by the job (plate names), and render_job reads a still cover's pixels (over 72% flat stops).

  the six Reels before 9 Nov 07:00 Dubai ran lines, bold, glow, glow, glow, lines (three glows in a row; themes were

  Breaking, Famitsu and skin kits are outside it.
