# Release 2026-11-15 r1: one render per Reel, server checks

Card adv-20261115-one-render-server-checks, approved by Mohammad (15 Nov, 13:57 Dubai: "if the server can do it, focus on
that so the director does not check multiple times, especially for repeated things"). Case: Trafficity, POPUCOM, Duck
Side of the Moon, Dreamlight Valley and Ranger's Path each rendered three times on 15 Nov (05:40Z to 07:30Z), qc pass
every time; Production 2 hit its 5-hour limit at 07:46Z. No change to look, templates or sound.

## Changed paths

- templates/textrules.py: A, `head_overlap` (no story line before 3.30 s, when the headline band starts to fade; names
  the earliest start and how much longer the shots before it must be; showcase, Famitsu, breaking and persist.head
  exempt). Spoken: `spoken_missing` (no "spoken", or not 2 to 3 sentences) and `spoken_order` (the lines, in order, are
  that paragraph: a word may be left out, none added; و and ف split off, diacritics and alef forms folded).
- templates/buildgate.py: the gate refuses a job with lines and no spoken paragraph.
- templates/shotgate.py (new): B, C, D on the server's own copy. B: a shot may not start more than 0.25 s before the
  picture (the video's start time minus the file's, the seek coordinates reel.py uses), cross a scene cut, or sit on a
  letterbox (over 8 rows and 1.5% of the height); each refusal names the nearest clean start. C: the subject the
  window frames (YOLOv8n: people, vehicles, animals; at the start, middle and end) may not leave the 9:16 window, nor
  be cut by its edge at the first frame; the refusal gives the longest safe length. D: `ground_pick`, the calmest
  window of a card's ground stretch. Waivers after looking: cut_ok, letterbox_ok, drift_ok.
- templates/render_job.py: shot gate in `check` (so prerender and t0make) and in `run` before anything is built
  (breaking exempt); D in the card build (`"ground_seek": "fixed"` keeps the seek as written; the render prints the
  pick).
- templates/shotpick.py: the prerender shots read returns the picture start and cut list and drops stretches before the
  picture or letterboxed. templates/t0make.py: shot 0 holds the headline (3.5 s), line 1 only after the band clears,
  "spoken": TODO.
- templates/presubmit.py: E, with qc pass a FIX needs `--reason` wrong_subject | graphic | unreadable_text |
  factual_error, one per story; the second is refused ("no third render"); each send-back goes to the story file and
  to cards/YYYY-MM/<name>-qc.json under director_sendbacks.
- templates/qc.py: look frames for every Reel; director_sendbacks carried over when the server rewrites qc.json;
  `qc.py rerender <job>` refuses a render after a director PASS, or a third render after a send-back, unless the job
  names "rerender": {"reason", "note"}. .github/workflows/render-live.yml runs it after the jobsha check.
- ops/sendbacks.py (new): F, send-backs by reason over 7 days; a reason seen 3 times writes a build-desk card.
- templates/test_onerender_checks.py (new, 13 tests); test_onerender.py and test_qcfail.py fixtures.
- CLAUDE.md, STYLE.md, newsroom/delegation.md (Director limit card), ops/production-setup.md (Production 2 line 15).

calibration the refusals of v1 and v2 above are unchanged.
