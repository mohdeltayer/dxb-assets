# Release 2026-11-17 r3: The Game Awards nominees in event style

Mohammad, 17 Nov 2026: "on X and Threads, rapid individual video posts per category as each is ready; on Instagram, one
combined Reel with the main categories plus one Reel for Game of the Year." Additive: no live template changes behaviour
for any existing job.

## What changes

- templates/cardgfx.py: new card kind `nominees`: the category title, 3 to 6 nominees in the order read, each with an
  optional studio note and box tile. No rank badge, no bar (a nominee list is not a ranking). Rows enter one by one from
  the top (0.55 s apart) in the numbers and list templates. Six rows use 112 px rows so the plate ends above the story
  lines (y 1258). A title that wraps to two lines drops its note. Latin titles are drawn left to right ("007 First Light"
  had flipped to "First Light 007" in the first test).
- templates/tgakit.py:
  - `cat <slug> <key|Name::note::tile,...> [--ground KEY] [--title AR]`: the X and Threads video for one category. Hook on
    a nominee's footage under «المرشحون لجائزة / <category>», the nominees card on the same game's moving footage, the
    ceremony date on a closing picture; about 12 s plus the end card. It writes jobs/live/tga-2026-cat-<slug>.json, its X
    and Threads texts and a story file, and records the category in the kit.
  - `main <slug,...> [--hook KEY]`: the Instagram Reel of the main categories, one card per category, each on one of its
    own nominees' footage, built from the categories already written (the same rows, so the posts never disagree).
  - Arabic names for 24 categories (`CATEGORIES`); a nominee read with only an item stretch borrows its hook and close
    from its other clean stretches.

## Tests (GitHub runner, branch build/tga-event-style, then main)

- tga-dry-cat-art: 6 rows, rendered in 165 s, 14.0 s with the end card; ground for the card is the PBZ trailer at its
  calmest 6 s (the scene is dark).
- tga-dry-main-categories: 3 categories, 683 s on the 4-core runner (25.5 s); every card on its own game's footage. Its
  cover opened on a near-black RE9 frame: `--hook` added.
- tga-dry-cat-actionadventure from main: checks the left-to-right fix.
