# Release 2026-11-10 r4: coverage over caution

Asked by Mohammad, 9 Nov 2026 about 21:30 Dubai: "I want to fix the strict rules in production 2 because it's
limiting the coverage and I don't understand the logic behind it. It could've checked Nintendo Everything to verify."

## What went wrong

A Little to the Left's Switch 2 Edition (shadow drop, 9 Nov) had a publisher press release on GamesPress and a Nintendo
Everything report, and was on the board as a T0 handoff from 15:57 Dubai. Production 2's Haiku scout dropped it twice:
15:35 Dubai as "single outlet" (not a listed reason), 16:35 as "no source found". The scout's sweep reads the newest posts
of a few newsroom front pages (PS Blog, Xbox Wire, Nintendo US; Capcom 503, Bandai Namco 403, Square Enix 404) and does
not search for a story by name, so anything not on those front pages read as "no source". The same reading held Sea of
Remnants (date "only in the YouTube video"), Skydance and Bit Reactor ("held for a statement"); the holds were never
decided. The rules on paper (9 Nov: when in doubt publish with the honest label) were not strict; the scout's reading of
"source" was.

## What changes

1. `newsroom/sources.md`: primaries (company sites and store pages, official YouTube and X posts, press wires, filings,
   rating boards) and credible outlets (one is enough for تقرير); the search before any no_source; what is not a reason.
2. `newsroom/scout_file.py check` refuses: a rejected lead carrying `"handoff"`; no_source while a primary or credible
   link is in hand; no_source without `"searched"` (3 or more, one a web search); no_source or denied without
   `"confirmed_by": "editor"`; carried without `"waiting_for"`; a rejection note using a not-a-reason phrase.
3. `ops/desk_audit.py`: BAD-REJECTION for "single outlet", "no primary read", "no written primary", "only in the
   video", "Arabic-only", "held for a statement" in any rejection or hold.
4. Work order item 4: orders and handoffs are built, not judged; the search and source rule in one line; a previous
   run's rejection that used a not-a-reason phrase is marked for a re-check.
5. Production 2 line 9 in `ops/production-setup.md`; CLAUDE.md and STYLE.md section "Coverage over caution".

## Not changed

The four rejection reasons, the honest-label ladder, the excluded sources, the variety and spacing rules.
