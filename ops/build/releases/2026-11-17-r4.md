# Release 2026-11-17 r4: server speed C, D, E

Card adv-20261117-server-speed-for-p2 (Mohammad, 17 Nov 2026), parts C, D and E. A and B went out in r2.

## C. The job stays on main after a render
- render.yml and render-live.yml move the rendered job, with the server's fixes, to `jobs/done/<name>.json` in the render
  commit (was: deleted). The commit body names it (`job: jobs/done/<name>.json`) and qc.json carries `job_path`.
- `templates/jobpath.py` resolves `done` (jobs/done first, then live, render, test); prerender also looks in jobs/done.
- `presubmit.py director` finds the job without a path: from the Reel's qc.json (`job_path`, else its job name) or a bare
  name. Case: on 17 Nov the Director read the Xbox sale job from an old commit.
- jobs/done is never watched by a workflow, so nothing re-renders from it.

## D. Minutes per stage
- `render_job.run` records `pipeline` (fetch, shots, checks, render, after seconds) on its render-times entry and prints
  `render_job: pipeline:`.
- `templates/stagetimes.py` assembles queue, setup, fetch, shots, checks, render, after and upload: one line in the
  render commit body, `stage_minutes` in qc.json, and the work order's new section 7 (the last five renders, with the
  slowest stage named). The Publer upload step times itself and adds `upload_secs` in the receipt commit.

## E. Unfinished work handed to the next run
- `ops/backoffice/dispatch/incomplete.py open|close|show` keeps one row per story in dxb-queue `desk/incomplete.json`
  (story, next step, job, render run id, slot; no Publer ids or links).
- keeper.yml reads it; the work order lists open rows in a new section 1b, right after the alarms ("pick up first"), and
  raises an `incomplete` alarm for a row open 2 hours. The board lines stay as they are; the file is the machine-readable
  half. Production 2 line 16 in `ops/production-setup.md`.

## Tests

  upload writes, last five, unfinished rows, refusals of ids and links, work order section and alarm, keeper wiring).
- Regression: test_workorder, test_keeper, test_presubmit, test_qcfail, test_onerender, test_onerender_checks,
  test_autofix, test_speed all pass. The three test_buildgate failures predate this release.

  the first live render after the merge is the live check.
