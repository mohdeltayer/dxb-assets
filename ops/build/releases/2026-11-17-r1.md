# Release 2026-11-17 r1: the gates fix, not refuse

Card adv-20261117-gates-fix-not-refuse, approved by Mohammad. Case: Production 2 built 3 Reels in 10 hours on 16 and
17 Nov because each gate refused one fault per pass (Xbox White Friday sale: nine job versions; Galactic Racer: a credit
too wide, then a smear flag on our own cut). No change to look, templates, sound or the published post. Every fix is on
by default; `"autofix": false` in a job turns the fixes off (B); Famitsu and breaking are outside it.

## What changes for production

- The server fixes what it can, and says so. A render may rewrite the job on main (render-live) after it fixed it,
  whether the render then passed or was refused for what was left: pull the job before editing it again.
- One pass lists everything. prerender (text, full) and the render's text step return every refusal at once, each with
  the value that passes, and a "FIXED" list of what the server changed.
- `"shots": "auto"`, or `[file, "auto", secs, focus]` in a written list: the server picks the shot times (C).
- buildgate and every text check run on the server through `prerender.yml` mode text (E): no local Pillow is needed.
- `presubmit.py intent` needs `--slot <ISO time of the post>` on every post (F).

## Changed paths

- templates/autofix.py (new), B: shots (a shot crossing a scene cut or past its safe length is trimmed, the time given
  to the next or previous shot if that stays clean; a subject cut by the 9:16 edge takes a focus that keeps it in, else
  the nearest clean stretch within 5 s), a line under the headline (the shot before it is lengthened so the line starts
  at the earliest allowed second), a credit too wide (one size down, then two lines, never below 30 px, the break at a
  middot and never before a Latin word), the textrules mechanical fixes. Each change goes to `autofix.json`, qc.json
  `autofixed` and `job_after_autofix`. What is left is refused with what was tried. Card grounds already take the
  calmest window (15 Nov, D of that card); unchanged.
- templates/render_job.py, A and B: `check(job, collect=[])` runs every stage and keeps every failure; shot gate
  autofix before refusing; text step `--fix`; the credit drawn as reel draws it (size, band, two lines).
- templates/reel.py: a credit may carry "\n" (two rows; a low credit grows upward; each row checked for width).
- templates/autoshots.py (new), C: expand (no footage: placeholders so text checks see the timeline) and fill (on the
  server, from templates/shotpick.py's clean stretches, in source order, each near its even share of the trailer,
  centred subjects preferred). Called by render_job run and prerender full; textrules and buildgate expand.
- templates/decodecheck.py and render_job.py, D: the smear check measures each frame against its own shot (and the end
  card as its own), so a busy clip between two stills no longer reads as a smear. Battlefield 1 v1's source damage
  (5.70 to 6.10 s) is still caught.
- templates/prerender.py, .github/workflows/prerender.yml, E: text and full mode run buildgate and every text check on
  the server, print every refusal and every fix in the summary.
- .github/workflows/render-live.yml and render.yml: AUTOFIX_JSON and AUTOFIX_JOB; render-live writes the fixed job back
  to main (only if main's job is still the one rendered) and keeps the fixes in qc.json.
- F: templates/presubmit.py (every intent stores its slot; `--slot` required), ops/backoffice/keeper/workorder.py
  (`posts_from_logs`: published.jsonl by the X link's time, submissions.jsonl by an intent's slot once scheduled;
  merged with the commitments and ledger), keeper.py (`logs_last` for coverage), .github/workflows/keeper.yml (reads
  both logs). The keeper had read only commitments.json, which presubmit never wrote: "GAP 1047 min" while eight posts
  went out. Posts made before this release have no slot in the log, so the gap shrinks as posts go out under it. The
  server's own Publer read (a key file on dxb-render-1, root) is still the fuller fix and stays a board question.
